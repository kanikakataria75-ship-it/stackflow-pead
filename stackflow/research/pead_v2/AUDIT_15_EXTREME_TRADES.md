## 4. Outlier Removal Sensitivity Tests

To test whether performance collapses if the best trades are excluded, daily portfolio equity was independently simulated removing the top 1, top 5, and top 10 winning trades:

| Portfolio Scenario | Trades Taken | Strategy CAGR (%) | Delta vs. Full Strategy | Benchmark CAGR (%) | Net Excess CAGR (%) | Strategy Survives? |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Baseline (All Trades)** | **579** | **+16.83%** | 0.00 pp | **+11.05%** | **+5.78%** | **YES** |
| **Drop Top 1 Trade** (`NLCINDIA`) | 578 | **+16.77%** | -0.05 pp | +11.05% | **+5.72%** | **YES** |
| **Drop Top 5 Trades** | 574 | **+16.45%** | -0.38 pp | +11.05% | **+5.40%** | **YES** |
| **Drop Top 10 Trades** | 569 | **+16.22%** | -0.61 pp | +11.05% | **+5.17%** | **YES** |

### Audit Finding:
The strategy shows exceptional robustness to outlier removal. Excluding the 10 most profitable trades of the entire five-year span reduces the annualized return by only **0.61 percentage points** (from +16.83% to +16.22%). The strategy retains a **+5.17% annual excess return** over the Nifty 500. This confirms the edge is broadly distributed across the 579 trades.
