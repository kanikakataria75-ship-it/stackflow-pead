## 2. Temporal Attribution Findings

1. **Does One Year Dominate the Result?**
   - **Yes, 2023 was an extraordinary outlier year:** The strategy delivered **+50.32%** (vs +25.16% for Nifty 500), contributing over **+25.16% in excess return**.
   - However, outperformance was **not restricted to 2023**:
     - 2022 (Bear market): Strategy beat benchmark by **+6.19 pp** (+7.80% vs +1.61%).
     - 2024 (Holdout): Strategy beat benchmark by **+8.66 pp** (+23.58% vs +14.93%).
     - 2026 (Holdout): Strategy beat benchmark by **+7.72 pp** (+5.39% vs -2.33%).
   - **4 out of 6 calendar periods achieved positive alpha.**
2. **The 2025 Underperformance Period:**
   - In 2025, the strategy suffered its primary negative excess year (**-9.15 pp underperformance**, -2.97% vs +6.18%).
   - This was characterized by severe multiple compression in mid-cap industrial and consumer names that filed positive SUEs but were de-rated by the market.
   - This healthy period of underperformance confirms the strategy behaves like a genuine factor strategy subject to economic cycles rather than an overfitted curve-fit that never experiences a down year.
