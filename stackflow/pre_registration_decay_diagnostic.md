# Pre-Registration — Edge Decay Diagnostic (D1 / D2 / D3)

**Written:** 2026-09-21, BEFORE gathering any evidence for D1, D2 or D3.
**Type:** EXPLANATORY research. Not a tradeable-signal test.

No forward-return claim is made here, so there is deliberately **no confirm/kill pair**
in the trading sense. What is pre-registered instead is **what evidence would make each
explanation more or less plausible**, fixed in advance so the write-up cannot become a
post-hoc story fitted to whatever turns up.

## The fact being explained

Phase 1c found the frozen Layer 1 cell (N=20/M=90, Panel C) roughly **4x weaker** in
2016-2026 (**-0.380%**) than in 2005-2015 (**-1.550%**). Dispersion-normalised 5-year
blocks: -0.134, -0.150, **-0.050, -0.052** - a **step around 2015**, not a gradual fade,
stable across two subsequent independent blocks.

## D1 - Rising institutional/algo participation in SECTOR-level trading

**More plausible if:** a clear, dated rise in sectoral/thematic index-fund and ETF AUM,
NSE algo/co-location turnover share, or FII/DII sector-flow responsiveness that
**roughly coincides with or precedes the ~2015 step**.
**Less plausible if:** no 2015-adjacent inflection; or an inflection clearly later or
earlier than the observed step; or no clear trend at all.

## D2 - General market efficiency increase (not sector-specific)

**More plausible, relative to D1, if:** efficiency proxies (bid-ask spreads, etc.) show a
**broad market-wide** shift in the same period, and/or published research shows
comparable post-2015 compression in **other, unrelated** lag-based anomalies
(stock momentum, PEAD). Sources must be cited; no number is to be invented if none is found.

## D3 - Structural artifact of the panel itself

**More plausible if:** the ~2015 step **lines up cleanly** with panel widening or index
provider methodology changes.
**Less plausible if:** the step **predates** any panel change. Phase 1c already noted
panel widening happened "in the final block, after the step". If confirmed precisely,
this is to be reported as **evidence against D3 explicitly**, not left to inference.

## Decisive internal test, specified in advance

D3 is the only candidate fully answerable from data already cached. The decisive test:
**re-run the frozen cell on a CONSTANT panel** - only sectors present continuously since
2006 - and check whether the step survives. If the step persists on a panel that never
changed composition, D3 is ruled out as the primary cause, whatever the timing shows.

## Rules for this phase

1. **No re-tuning or re-selection** of the frozen Layer 1 config on anything found here.
2. **No explanation is treated as proven.** This is diagnostic evidence toward a Layer 2
   design decision, not a validated finding.
3. **No fabricated figures.** Any metric not actually obtainable is reported as
   "not obtainable with available sources". Paid/proprietary-only data is noted as
   helpful and skipped, not blocked on.
4. **"Inconclusive" is an acceptable and expected verdict** for any of D1/D2/D3 where the
   available data does not discriminate. A preferred story is not to be forced.
5. Timeboxed to public sources answerable with reasonable effort.
